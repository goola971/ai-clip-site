Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

AI Clip is a desktop app. Your videos, settings and account tokens stay on your computer. The developer does not run a server that collects your data and does not sell or share personal data.

Data the app uses

  • Videos and project files you import or create — stored locally in your Windows profile.
  • TikTok data, only after you connect your account with TikTok Login Kit: your open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and permission to upload and publish the videos you choose (video.upload, video.publish). Access and refresh tokens are stored locally and used only to publish on your behalf.
  • YouTube data, only after you connect your Google account: channel name and public statistics of the videos you published, to show their performance.
  • API keys you enter for AI services (Groq, Gemini, ElevenLabs, YouTube Data, Twitch, Kick) — stored locally, never displayed back in full.

How data is shared

Data is sent only to the services you choose to use, directly from your computer: TikTok and YouTube when you publish or refresh statistics, and the AI providers you configured when you generate scripts, voices or subtitles. Each service processes data under its own privacy policy (for example the TikTok Privacy Policy).

Retention and deletion

Everything is kept on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect TikTok or YouTube in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and revoke access from your TikTok or Google account settings. Uninstalling the app and deleting the “AI Clip” folder in your Windows profile removes all local data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the platforms you use).

Contact
