Create, publish and track your short videos.

AI Clip is a Windows desktop studio for creators. Turn a long video into vertical Shorts with voice-over and subtitles, publish them to YouTube and TikTok, and see what performs best — all from your own computer.

See what it does

Create

Cut a passage, reframe it to 9:16, add an AI voice-over or automatic subtitles in several languages.

Series

Split a long video into numbered episodes and parts, ready to publish as a series.

Publish

Connect your own YouTube and TikTok accounts and publish or schedule your videos with titles, descriptions and privacy you choose.

Track

Follow views, likes and comments, and get recommendations on what to create next and when to post.

How AI Clip uses TikTok

  1. You click “Connect TikTok” in the app and sign in on TikTok’s own page (Login Kit). AI Clip never sees your password.
  2. AI Clip reads your basic profile (display name and avatar) to show which account is connected.
  3. When you choose to publish, AI Clip shows a preview, your TikTok nickname, and lets you pick the privacy level and interaction settings before sending the video with the Content Posting API.
  4. Access tokens are stored only on your computer. You can disconnect at any time from the app or from your TikTok settings.
